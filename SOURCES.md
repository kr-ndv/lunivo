Lunivo is an independent directory. It is not affiliated with CBSE, NCERT, the Ministry of Education, or any listed service.

## Original directory
The original CBSE Library external-links menu shown by the project owner:
https://cbseacademic.nic.in/library/index.html

Legacy names transcribed from that page include CBSE Academic, NCERT, MHRD, e-GyanKosh, Digital Library of India, National Science Digital Library, CBSE News Digest, CENBOSEC, Universal Digital Library, International Children's Digital Library, US Link, JSTOR, Internet Archive, Khan Academy and MIT OpenCourseWare.
